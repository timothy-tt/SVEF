What CMS & Pages does

This is where the SVEF team edits the website itself, without asking a developer.

  • Static pages: Home, About us, Activities, FAQ, Contact and Privacy / GDPR. Title, body and status, in English and Vietnamese.
  • Marketing publishing: Blog posts and Case studies are written and published from here, and go live in the matching section of the public website.
  • Editable after publishing: the author can reopen any page or article later and change the content, the SEO fields, the author and the publish date. No developer, no release.

Manage static & dynamic content without code changes. Bilingual EN/VN (BRD BR-W-01/02/03).
