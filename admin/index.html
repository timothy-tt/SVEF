What CMS & Pages does

The SVEF team can directly manage and update website content through this CMS, without requiring support or a new release process.

  • Static pages: Home, About us, Activities, FAQ, Contact and Privacy / GDPR. Title, body and status, in English and Vietnamese.
  • Editable after publishing: any published page or article can be reopened and updated at any time: content, SEO fields, author, and publish date. All changes go live immediately.

What Event Management does

Every SVEF event lives here as one record, created once and used by the website and the app alike.

  • The record holds the basics: name, dates, format, location and description. Publishing it puts the event on the website.
  • Each event also carries its app content: the agenda and its sessions, speakers, sponsors, the registrant list, 1-to-1 meeting bookings and push notifications. Open it with the App content button on the event's row.
