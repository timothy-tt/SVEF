What CMS & Pages does

The SVEF team can directly manage and update website content through this CMS, without requiring support or a new release process.

  • Static pages: Home, About us, Activities, FAQ, Contact and Privacy / GDPR. Title, body and status, in English and Vietnamese.
  • Editable after publishing: any published page or article can be reopened and updated at any time: content, SEO fields, author, and publish date. All changes go live immediately.

What Event Management does

Every event lives here, whether SVEF runs it or a partner proposes it. One record, created once.

  • Partner-proposed events arrive in the approval queue. Once approved they are published to the website like any other event.
  • Events organised by SVEF also carry app content: the agenda and its sessions, speakers, sponsors, the registrant list, 1-to-1 meeting bookings and push notifications. Open it with the App content button on the event's row.
  • The website shows the basic record plus the link through. Partner events do not appear in the app.
