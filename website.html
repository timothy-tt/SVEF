About us

A forum built for two economies

The Swiss-Viet Economic Forum is the leading platform fostering economic cooperation between Switzerland and Vietnam.

Our mission

SVEF connects businesses, institutions and policymakers to unlock trade, investment and innovation between Switzerland and Vietnam. We host forums, lead trade missions, provide market-entry support and advocate for member interests. This platform brings our membership, events and resources together in one professional, bilingual experience.
