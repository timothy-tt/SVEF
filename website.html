About us

A forum built for two economies

The Swiss-Viet Economic Forum is the leading platform fostering economic cooperation between Switzerland and Vietnam.

Our mission

SVEF exists so that a Swiss manufacturer and a Vietnamese distributor can find each other, understand each other and sign something.

SVEF connects businesses, institutions and policymakers to unlock trade, investment and innovation between Switzerland and Vietnam.

Everything the secretariat does falls into one of four programme groups: forums and seminars, trade missions, market entry support, and policy advocacy. The membership decides the agenda, the secretariat delivers it.

The forum in numbers

How the forum came about

It started as an informal supper club of Swiss executives posted to Ho Chi Minh City and Vietnamese founders who had studied in Zurich and Lausanne. The free trade negotiations turned that circle into an institution with a secretariat, a members register and an annual forum.

Two small, open, export-driven economies eight thousand kilometres apart have more in common than either expects. Our job is to shorten that distance. Trần Quốc Anh · Chair of the SVEF steering committee
